# Circuit access route: central Pattaya → Bira Circuit

Generated by `tools/circuit/access_corridor.py tools/circuit/config/bira.json` from
`data/osm/raw/bira_corridor_2026-10-18.json`. That file is OSM highways (motorway to unclassified) in the bbox
12.900,100.880,12.990,101.022, fetched with query `data/osm/queries/bira_corridor.overpassql`. The script also uses
the service roads from the circuit download.

## Real route (OSM, shortest travel time with class speeds)
**Outbound, 15.09 km:** Pattaya Klang Rd (east end of the central slice) → Sukhumvit Rd (trunk, about 300 m north)
→ Porn Prapa Nimit Rd ("Country Club Road") → Sukhumvit Pattaya 47 → Thammasat Rd (CBI.1095, dual one-way
carriageways) → Soi Mabprachun Bon 1 → CBI.2081 roundabout → CBI.2081 → **facility junction**, OSM node 1457068485.
The Bira service road (way 132455221) starts there and runs to the gate.

**Return, 15.11 km:** the same roads on the opposite carriageways, then Sukhumvit southbound and a trunk_link onto
Pattaya Klang Rd. The search is limited to a 120 m band around the outbound route. Without that band, U-turn rules
and one-way pairs send it around Highway 36, which is a different road.

**Alternative, not generated:** Pattaya Nuea Rd → Sukhumvit north → the Pattaya–Rayong bypass (Highway 36) → CBI.2081.
That is 22.3 km and needs a U-turn on Highway 36 because the circuit is on the far side of the divided highway.
Wikipedia describes Bira as being "on Highway 36". This route is the obvious later addition for an express-road
approach.

Which route most real visitors drive is **unverified**. Ours is the shortest by the model, not a survey.

## Corridor graph
- File: `build/bira/corridor_graph_game.json` (plus `corridor_graph_source.json` in metres). It uses the **same
  schema** as `build/central_pattaya/graph_game.json`: `origin_utm`, `compression` 0.5, `meters_per_stud` 0.28,
  `nodes` {osmNodeId: [x, z]}, and `edges` {id, way, class, tier, name, name_en, oneway, lanes, junction, width,
  sidewalk, a, b, coastal, pts}. Each edge also has `corridor: "bira"`, and the file has a `join_nodes` field.
- The transform is the city's: `(UTM - origin_utm) * 0.5 / 0.28`, z = -north, Chaikin ×3 with fixed ends, resampled
  every 6 studs. Lane widths and class tiers are the same (trunk = express, secondary/tertiary = main,
  unclassified = soi).
- Size: 35 edges, 33 nodes. Classes: tertiary 18, trunk 7, secondary 7, trunk_link 2, unclassified 1. One way is
  about 27,000 studs, roughly 2.8 min at 160 studs/s.
- **Join node with the central slice: 423748313**, the east end of Pattaya Klang Road. It is in both graphs. The
  city puts it at (3006.33, 488.81) and the corridor at (3006.32, 488.82), only 0.01 stud apart. No other corridor
  node is in the city graph.
- **Join node with the venue: 1457068485**, the corridor's last node, at map-local (24,791.4, 2,893.4).

### Merging (for the leader / the city-expansion owner)
- Append the corridor edges to the city graph and dedupe nodes by OSM id. Node ids are OSM node ids in both files.
- If `pattaya_wide` also covers Pattaya Klang east of the slice or Sukhumvit, those edges come from the same OSM ways.
  Dedupe by `way` plus node ids, and keep the city version.
- The corridor runs through empty world (no terrain or buildings outside the slice). PattayaBuilder needs ground
  under it, which is a decision for the city pipeline.
- Dual carriageways (Sukhumvit, Thammasat Rd) are two one-way edges. At x0.5 with 13-stud lanes they may overlap,
  which is the same known issue as in the city.

## Transition from x0.5 corridor to the 1:1 venue
- At J the corridor is at its compressed position. From J a **straight transition road** (26 studs wide, flat at
  y = 1) runs in the real access direction for **200 studs** to the gate, where the 1:1 facility is anchored.
- Change of scale: positions are continuous everywhere, so nothing jumps. What changes is the distance per metre:
  1.786 studs/m on the corridor (up to J) and 3.571 studs/m inside the venue (from the gate). The transition road
  is the seam. Its 200 studs stand for only 6.2 real metres plus the space that opens up when the venue is drawn
  twice as large as its surroundings. It is not a real distance.
- Its length is found automatically: the shortest length, at least `transition_min_studs`, at which the venue
  outline, the 40 × 30 m entrance plaza and the road itself stay 30 studs clear of every corridor edge.
- The plaza sits on the transition road just before the gate (gameplay parking and arrival point). The gate arch
  shows only the descriptive venue name.
- Data: `access` in `roblox/data/circuits/bira.luau` (pts, width 26, joinNode 1457068485, scaleFrom 1.786,
  scaleTo 3.571, realLengthM 6.2, lengthStuds 200).

## Built and tested by the leader (2026-10-18)
- `tools/circuit/export_corridor.py` turns the corridor graph into a `PattayaBuilder.Extend()` dataset
  (`build/bira/roblox_corridor/`: manifest + 16 chunk modules, 620 road segments, 6 new junction discs, flat ground
  strips along the road clipped to stay outside the slice frame and short of the venue) and a merged runtime road
  graph (`graph.luau`: the 463 city nodes / 614 edges first, unchanged, then 32 corridor nodes / 35 edges;
  `baseNodes`/`baseEdges` keep sprint, challenge and boss destinations inside the city, and `osmIds` lets the GPS
  address junctions by OSM id).
- One-way corridor edges get no sidewalk: on the dual carriageways (Thammasat Rd) each carriageway's sidewalk sat
  on the other's roadway at ×0.5 (252 of 1,385 probes hit a sidewalk).
- Studio: `Extend({folder = "PattayaDataCorridor"})` built the 16 chunks in 22 s (2,782 parts) with Ground terrain
  under them. 1,385 raycast probes every 25 studs along the corridor all hit road at y = 1, from the city join
  (`Chunk_5_0 Pattaya Klang Road`) to the circuit's `TransitionRoad`, whose nearest part is 27 studs from the
  corridor's last node.
- **GPS** (`PattayaGPSServer` / `PattayaGPSClient`, key G): routes on the merged graph with
  `PattayaRoute.routeTo`, which snaps the car to the nearest point on any edge and seeds Dijkstra from both ends
  of that edge (snapping to junctions only lost the route mid-corridor, where edges are thousands of studs long).
  The client draws the next 2,500 studs as a cyan line, an arrow over the car and the remaining route distance.
- **Drive test** (physics-driven autopilot following the GPS line, traffic made non-solid for the run): from the
  Pattaya start grid to "You have arrived: Bira Circuit" in **206 s**, 402 probes under the car all on road, car
  upright (min UpVector.Y 0.99), at most 7 studs off the GPS line. Route 8.1 km in game (28,942 studs).
- Not done: the corridor has no buildings, props or traffic; a return trip was routed (2,832 studs from
  mid-corridor back to Pattaya Klang) but not driven; the Highway 36 alternative is not generated.
