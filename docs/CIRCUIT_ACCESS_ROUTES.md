# Circuit access route: central Pattaya → Bira Circuit

Generated by `tools/circuit/access_corridor.py tools/circuit/config/bira.json` from
`data/osm/raw/bira_corridor_2026-10-18.json`. That file is OSM highways (motorway to unclassified) in the bbox
12.900,100.880,12.990,101.022, fetched with query `data/osm/queries/bira_corridor.overpassql`. The script also uses
the service roads from the circuit download.

## Real route (OSM, shortest travel time with class speeds)
**Outbound, 15.09 km:** Pattaya Klang Rd (east end of the central slice) → Sukhumvit Rd (trunk, about 300 m north)
→ Porn Prapa Nimit Rd ("Country Club Road") → Sukhumvit Pattaya 47 → Thammasat Rd (CBI.1095, dual one-way
carriageways) → Soi Mabprachun Bon 1 → CBI.2081 roundabout → CBI.2081 → **facility junction**, OSM node 1457068485.
The Bira service road (way 132455221) starts there and runs to the gate.

**Return, 15.11 km:** the same roads on the opposite carriageways, then Sukhumvit southbound and a trunk_link onto
Pattaya Klang Rd. The search is limited to a 120 m band around the outbound route. Without that band, U-turn rules
and one-way pairs send it around Highway 36, which is a different road.

**Alternative, not generated:** Pattaya Nuea Rd → Sukhumvit north → the Pattaya–Rayong bypass (Highway 36) → CBI.2081.
That is 22.3 km and needs a U-turn on Highway 36 because the circuit is on the far side of the divided highway.
Wikipedia describes Bira as being "on Highway 36". This route is the obvious later addition for an express-road
approach.

Which route most real visitors drive is **unverified**. Ours is the shortest by the model, not a survey.

## Corridor graph
- File: `build/bira/corridor_graph_game.json` (plus `corridor_graph_source.json` in metres). It uses the **same
  schema** as `build/central_pattaya/graph_game.json`: `origin_utm`, `compression` 0.5, `meters_per_stud` 0.28,
  `nodes` {osmNodeId: [x, z]}, and `edges` {id, way, class, tier, name, name_en, oneway, lanes, junction, width,
  sidewalk, a, b, coastal, pts}. Each edge also has `corridor: "bira"`, and the file has a `join_nodes` field.
- The transform is the city's: `(UTM - origin_utm) * 0.5 / 0.28`, z = -north, Chaikin ×3 with fixed ends, resampled
  every 6 studs. Lane widths and class tiers are the same (trunk = express, secondary/tertiary = main,
  unclassified = soi).
- Size: 35 edges, 33 nodes. Classes: tertiary 18, trunk 7, secondary 7, trunk_link 2, unclassified 1. One way is
  about 27,000 studs, roughly 2.8 min at 160 studs/s.
- **Join node with the central slice: 423748313**, the east end of Pattaya Klang Road. It is in both graphs. The
  city puts it at (3006.33, 488.81) and the corridor at (3006.32, 488.82), only 0.01 stud apart. No other corridor
  node is in the city graph.
- **Join node with the venue: 1457068485**, the corridor's last node, at map-local (24,791.4, 2,893.4).

### Merging (for the leader / the city-expansion owner)
- Append the corridor edges to the city graph and dedupe nodes by OSM id. Node ids are OSM node ids in both files.
- If `pattaya_wide` also covers Pattaya Klang east of the slice or Sukhumvit, those edges come from the same OSM ways.
  Dedupe by `way` plus node ids, and keep the city version.
- The corridor runs through empty world (no terrain or buildings outside the slice). PattayaBuilder needs ground
  under it, which is a decision for the city pipeline.
- Dual carriageways (Sukhumvit, Thammasat Rd) are two one-way edges. At x0.5 with 13-stud lanes they may overlap,
  which is the same known issue as in the city.

## Transition from x0.5 corridor to the 1:1 venue
- At J the corridor is at its compressed position. From J a **straight transition road** (26 studs wide, flat at
  y = 1) runs in the real access direction for **200 studs** to the gate, where the 1:1 facility is anchored.
- Change of scale: positions are continuous everywhere, so nothing jumps. What changes is the distance per metre:
  1.786 studs/m on the corridor (up to J) and 3.571 studs/m inside the venue (from the gate). The transition road
  is the seam. Its 200 studs stand for only 6.2 real metres plus the space that opens up when the venue is drawn
  twice as large as its surroundings. It is not a real distance.
- Its length is found automatically: the shortest length, at least `transition_min_studs`, at which the venue
  outline, the 40 × 30 m entrance plaza and the road itself stay 30 studs clear of every corridor edge.
- The plaza sits on the transition road just before the gate (gameplay parking and arrival point). The gate arch
  shows only the descriptive venue name.
- Data: `access` in `roblox/data/circuits/bira.luau` (pts, width 26, joinNode 1457068485, scaleFrom 1.786,
  scaleTo 3.571, realLengthM 6.2, lengthStuds 200).

## Built and tested by the leader (2026-10-18)
- `tools/circuit/export_corridor.py` turns the corridor graph into a `PattayaBuilder.Extend()` dataset
  (`build/bira/roblox_corridor/`: manifest + 16 chunk modules, 620 road segments, 6 new junction discs, flat ground
  strips along the road clipped to stay outside the slice frame and short of the venue) and a merged runtime road
  graph (`graph.luau`: the 463 city nodes / 614 edges first, unchanged, then 32 corridor nodes / 35 edges;
  `baseNodes`/`baseEdges` keep sprint, challenge and boss destinations inside the city, and `osmIds` lets the GPS
  address junctions by OSM id).
- One-way corridor edges get no sidewalk: on the dual carriageways (Thammasat Rd) each carriageway's sidewalk sat
  on the other's roadway at ×0.5 (252 of 1,385 probes hit a sidewalk).
- Studio: `Extend({folder = "PattayaDataCorridor"})` built the 16 chunks in 22 s (2,782 parts) with Ground terrain
  under them. 1,385 raycast probes every 25 studs along the corridor all hit road at y = 1, from the city join
  (`Chunk_5_0 Pattaya Klang Road`) to the circuit's `TransitionRoad`, whose nearest part is 27 studs from the
  corridor's last node.
- **GPS** (`PattayaGPSServer` / `PattayaGPSClient`, key G): routes on the merged graph with
  `PattayaRoute.routeTo`, which snaps the car to the nearest point on any edge and seeds Dijkstra from both ends
  of that edge (snapping to junctions only lost the route mid-corridor, where edges are thousands of studs long).
  The client draws the next 2,500 studs as a cyan line, an arrow over the car and the remaining route distance.
- **Drive test** (physics-driven autopilot following the GPS line, traffic made non-solid for the run): from the
  Pattaya start grid to "You have arrived: Bira Circuit" in **206 s**, 402 probes under the car all on road, car
  upright (min UpVector.Y 0.99), at most 7 studs off the GPS line. Route 8.1 km in game (28,942 studs).
- **Last leg:** from the corridor's end along the transition road and across the entrance plaza to the paddock ring
  (14 s, 38 probes: 15 on `TransitionRoad`, 22 on `Parking`, the first still on the corridor road), then the
  circuit menu accepted a Free Practice session at the paddock. So the directive's player journey (spawn → car →
  GPS → drive → paddock → activity) is driveable end to end.
- A straight line from the corridor's end to the ring (instead of the transition road) drops the car 4 studs onto
  terrain beside the road, from which the car cannot climb back (first attempt in the test). A low kerb or ground
  blending along the transition road would be a sensible later polish.
- Not done: the corridor has no buildings or props other than the street lights and signs below (traffic: see below); the return trip was driven on 2026-10-19 (see "Tested: return trip" below); the Highway 36 alternative is not generated; the world-map UI
  (menu with a map icon for the circuit) is still pending, GPS is key-driven for now.

## Tested: return trip from the paddock (leader, 2026-10-19, Studio Play)

- **Bug found and fixed:** the GPS refused "back to Central" from the Bira paddock with "No road nearby". The
  paddock (45002, 2796) is 233 studs from the nearest road-graph edge (the facility junction at 44791, 2893) and
  `PattayaRoute.routeTo` only snapped within 150 studs. `routeTo` now takes a `maxSnap` argument (default 150, so
  Street Sprint / Challenge / Road Fight are unchanged) and `PattayaGPSServer` passes `GPS_SNAP = 700`; the route then
  starts with a straight leg from the car to the nearest road. `PattayaTestKitServer.publishRouteTo` uses the same 700.
- **Drive:** physics-mode autopilot (horizontal velocity + yaw only) from the paddock to the Central start at
  140 studs/s: route 243 points / 26,835 studs; server probe 1,074/1,074 road; drive 200.7 s, 393 probes, 387 road,
  6 off (all in the first 3 s on the paddock apron / shoulder / verge while crossing to the transition road), then
  road all the way; `GPSUpdate arrived "Central Pattaya (start)"`; Heartbeat 60.
- **Known limit:** the straight first leg crosses the venue apron and a kerb; the car's up vector hit -1.00 once in
  those 3 s (it rolled on the kerb and recovered). A proper fix is to prefix the GPS route with the venue's paddock
  road polyline (CircuitBuilder knows it) instead of a straight line. Not done.
- Screenshot of the corridor at night (new sky, HUD accents): `docs/images/night_sky_v1_corridor.jpg`.

## Entrance, parking and paddock (team2/venue, 2026-10-18; built in code, NOT run in Studio)
Driving in from the corridor: at J the transition road starts. 40 studs later a green "BIRA CIRCUIT ->" road sign
stands on the left verge, then the destination pylon at the plaza entry, then the "<- PADDOCK" and
"P CAR PARK ->" signs. The plaza (30 × 40 m) is lined by 8 flag poles with plain coloured flags, and the gate arch
is 10 studs past the plaza.
- **Car park:** 120 × 64 studs, flush with the plaza's south side (right of the arrival direction), 24 marked bays
  of 10 × 22 studs in two rows facing a 20-stud aisle (`carPark`).
- **Paddock:** the pit garages and the apron behind them are in the infield, so every road there crosses the live
  track (OSM: an at-grade crossing at the west hairpin or tunnel 132455224). The players' paddock (`paddock`,
  130 × 80 studs, two team tents and a "PADDOCK" board) is therefore **outside the track, north of the plaza**.
  A 20-stud service road (`paddockRoad`) 52 studs long joins it to the plaza. The paddock point is 153 studs from
  the gate. The real service road 165168047 (gate → outside of T11 → grandstand) passes 100 studs east of it.
- **Paddock ring:** CircuitServer now replaces the compiled `entrance` CFrame with `def.paddock` when the definition
  has one. The ring/prompt, `Sessions.travel` and "moved to the paddock" all land there. `entrance` (the gate) stays
  the map/GPS destination. The 9-line change is at the top of `CircuitServer.server.luau`; the leader may move it
  into CircuitRegistry later.
- **Edges:** the transition road, plaza, car park, paddock and paddock road have verge slabs (top 1 stud under the
  road, 30 studs out) and 16-stud sloped shoulder planks on every edge, and the terrain is flattened to the same
  level and blended back over 64 studs. A car that leaves the road rolls down at most 1 stud onto flat ground and can
  drive back, instead of the 4-stud drop the leader found. The road tops are unchanged (still y = 1; plaza 1.02,
  car park 1.015, paddock road 0.96, paddock 1.01).
## Corridor life: traffic, street lights, direction signs (2026-10-18, data only, not run in Studio)
Config: `corridor_life` in `tools/circuit/config/bira.json`.
- **Traffic** (`tools/circuit/corridor_traffic.py`, run after `export_corridor.py` and the wide export): 3 loops on
  the directed corridor graph (`corridor_graph_game.json`; one-way edges only a → b, so the dual carriageways are
  driven out on one carriageway and back on the other), each from → to and back, offset into the left-hand lane with
  the same rule as `tools/mapgen/traffic.py`:
  | loop | studs | cars | speed |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Pattaya Klang east end (423748313) ↔ venue junction (1457068485) | 54,184 | 90 | 67.1 |
  | Sukhumvit Pattaya 47 start (605264874) ↔ Thammasat junction (1077826539) | 42,576 | 70 | 50.4 |
  | Thammasat junction (1077826539) ↔ venue junction | 7,032 | 11 | 61.8 |
  600 studs per car (the city uses 420), 50–68 studs/s. Output `build/bira/roblox_corridor/traffic.luau`: the 84 wide
  loops byte for byte, then the 3 corridor loops, same format (map-local studs; `PattayaTraffic.client` adds the
  ORIGIN) → `ReplicatedStorage.PattayaTraffic`. Also `build/bira/corridor_traffic.json`.
- **Street lights** (`export_corridor.py`): the city layout rule (every 110 studs on main/express edges, width/2 +
  sidewalk/2 from the centreline, facing the road, clear of every road and building). Two-way roads get both sides;
  one-way edges (one carriageway of a dual road) only the kerb side, left of travel, so the median is not lit twice.
  434 lights in the chunk modules' `lights` lists, assigned to the chunk of the edge they stand beside (a light's own
  position could fall in a chunk id the wide world uses). About 1,300 Parts and 217 SpotLights (every second lamp).
- **Direction signs** (`export_corridor.py`): manifest field `signs = {{x, z, dx, dz, text}}` (map-local, dx/dz =
  direction of travel). Placed where the REAL distance left along the route (game studs × 0.28 / 0.5) is the stated
  value, on the driver's left outside the sidewalk, only on stretches the corridor dataset builds:
  outbound "BIRA CIRCUIT 12 / 8 / 5 / 2 km", return "PATTAYA 12 / 7 / 3 km" (to the Pattaya Klang east end).
  `PattayaBuilder.buildRoadSigns` builds a grey post and a green board with a white SurfaceGui TextLabel (SmoothPlastic,
  original artwork) into `workspace.PattayaWorld.RoadSigns_bira_corridor`; `Extend` calls it when the manifest has
  `signs` and that model does not exist yet.
- Also new: `joints.luau` (13 joint discs, see `docs/ROAD_SYSTEM.md`). Studio steps: `docs/WORLD_CHUNKS.md`, "Road fix".
