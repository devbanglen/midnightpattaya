```
.venv\Scripts\python tools\circuit\bira_circuit.py    tools\circuit\config\bira.json
```
Outputs: `build/bira/bira.luau`, `build/bira/circuit.json` and the check image `build/bira/bira_check.png`.
`build/` is git-ignored, so a committed copy of the image is at `docs/images/bira_check.png` and the module is
copied to `roblox/data/circuits/bira.luau`. The builder is `roblox/src/ServerStorage/CircuitBuilder.luau`.

## Scale decision: 1:1 (not compressed)
- Studs per metre: **3.571** (0.28 m per stud, the same unit as the city, with compression 1.0). The city uses
  1.786 studs per metre because of its x0.5 distance compression.
- Why: our cars are about 7 × 16 studs, which is 2.0 × 4.5 m at 0.28 m/stud. That is a real car size, so at 1:1 a
  12 m track is 43 studs wide, about six car widths. Corner radii and braking distances stay true to the real place.
  In the city, roads are compressed in length but lanes stay full width (13 studs, about 3.6 m), so cars and lanes
  already use the 0.28 scale. The circuit just leaves out the length compression.
- Result: lap **8,521 studs** (3D) = 2,383 m. With the city cars' 160 studs/s top speed (about 161 km/h) a lap
  should take roughly 70–90 s. That is plausible for road cars at Bira but has **not been driven**.
- Vertical scale is 1.0: 23 m of relief becomes about 82 studs (y -26 to +55).

## Placement
- The corridor graph (city schema, x0.5) ends at the **facility junction** J: OSM node 1457068485 on CBI.2081.
  J sits exactly at its city-compressed position: world (44,791.4, 1, 2,893.4) = map-local (24,791.4, 2,893.4) +
  ORIGIN (20000, 0, 0).
- The real road from J to the gate is only 6.2 m long. If the 1:1 venue were hung off J directly, it would cover the
  x0.5 roundabout and Thammasat Road just west of J. Un-compressing the venue needs extra space, and a **straight
  transition road** provides it. It runs from J in the real access direction (J → track corner, bearing
  roughly east-southeast) to the gate. Its length is the shortest that clears every corridor road by 30 studs (the
  facility outline, the plaza and the road itself are all checked). That length is currently the 200-stud minimum.
  Its length is game slack, **not a real distance** (see `CIRCUIT_ACCESS_ROUTES.md`).
- The facility (track, pits, buildings, service roads, terrain) is placed at 1:1 around the gate, in its **real
  orientation** (north stays north).
- World footprint of the venue outline: x 44,735–48,049, z 2,117–3,987, surface y -26 to +55. The central slice
  covers x 18,535–23,006, so they do not overlap, and the corridor generator reports **no corridor edge overlapping
  the facility**. Teammates generating other areas (Jomtien/Naklua) should keep clear of x > 44,500,
  z 1,900–4,100. Note: at x ≈ 48,000, far from the world origin, physics precision is still fine (well under
  100k), but nobody has checked it in Play yet.
- Height reference: the smoothed track height nearest the gate (108.4 m DEM) is placed at y = 1, the city road top.
  The transition road and the plaza are flat at y = 1.

## Data interface (what teammates code against)
All coordinates are world studs, indices are 1-based, and `side = 1` means right of the driving direction. Fields:
`id`, `name`, `lengthStuds`, `studsPerMetre`, `direction`, `centerline` (x,y,z flat, closed, driving direction,
y = surface), `width` (per point), `trackWidth`, `startFinish.index`, `sectors`, `grid` ({x,y,z,dx,dz}),
`pitLane` (x,y,z flat), `pitLaneWidth`, `pitSide`, `pitEntryIndex`, `pitExitIndex`, `pitSpeedLimit` (studs/s),
`curbs` ({from,to,side}), `curbWidth`, `barrierRight`/`barrierLeft` (per point, studs from the centreline, 0 = no
barrier), `runoffDefault`, `brakeMarkers` ({index,dist,side,x,y,z}), `corners` ({from,to,turn,dir,radiusStuds}),
`buildings` ({kind,x,z,w,d,h,yaw,y,verified,source}; `yaw` is in degrees with the same convention as
PattayaBuilder, and `y` is the base height), `parking`, `entrance` ({x,y,z,dx,dz}), `access`
({pts, width, joinNode, corridorGraph, ...}), `serviceRoads`, `floodlights` ({x,y,z,dx,dz}), `ground`
(heightfield), `sources`, `verified`, `attribution`.

## Builder (CircuitBuilder.luau)
`require(ServerStorage.CircuitBuilder).Build(def, {terrain = true, lights = true})` replaces
`workspace.Circuit_<id>` and returns a short summary. Parts are anchored SmoothPlastic, and it calls `task.wait()`
every 200 parts.
- Track: slab parts 4 studs thick whose top faces pass through the centreline points. They are pitched with the
  slope and have no roll (Bira has no banking data). Nearly straight runs are merged: up to 60 studs per part, with
  a 3D tolerance of 0.08 studs. Each part is lengthened by the outer-edge gap of the bend plus 0.3 stud. Because
  neighbouring tops cross exactly at their shared point, a raycast wheel finds no step. Estimated parts: track ~456,
  run-off ~879, curbs ~860, edge lines ~900, barriers ~1,000–1,500, pit lane ~140, plus buildings, lights and boards.
  That is about **5–6k parts** (estimated, not measured).
- Run-off is one wide slab per segment under the track, 0.15 stud lower, so its top never rises above the asphalt.
  The pit lane top is 0.02 lower than the track and service roads 0.04 lower, so overlaps never create a lip.
- Curbs: alternating red/white 5-stud pieces with the top 0.1 stud above the asphalt. The inner edge overlaps the
  asphalt edge by 0.5 stud. Barriers are concrete walls 1.2 × 3.6 studs.
- Floodlights: a pole plus a `FloodLight` head part (attribute `FloodLight=true`) holding a `SpotLight` with
  `Enabled=false`, angle 100, range 60, aimed at the track and down. The Night Run system switches them on.
- Terrain: Grass→Air is cleared over the frame (in 512-stud tiles), then grass columns are filled per cell.

### Builder assumptions (check on the first Studio build)
1. **Not run yet.** The Luau compiles with `luau-compile` and passes `luau-analyze` (Roblox globals ignored), and
   the data module loads and passes index and size checks. Nobody has built it in Studio.
2. WedgePart orientation: the seating slope is assumed to face the part's Front (LookVector, toward the track). If
   the grandstand faces away, flip `front`.
3. Terrain: the "fill renders about 2 studs higher" offset comes from PattayaBuilder's measurement. Check that grass
   never shows through the track, especially on crests.
4. The data module is 165 KB. If inserting it with HttpService/`Source` hits a size limit, split `ground.h` into its
   own module.
5. Streaming: the grid is Persistent. Everything else uses the default streaming mode, and nothing has been tested
   with StreamingEnabled at this distance.
6. The paddock is reached from the gate by an at-grade crossing at the west hairpin (that is how OSM draws it) or by
   the OSM tunnel 132455224 (not built). For now the access road ends at the plaza by the gate.
