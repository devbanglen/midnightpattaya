# RayCity Research: How Racing Worked

Research date: 2026-10-18. RayCity (레이시티) shut down everywhere in 2013, so almost every source is a wiki, an old news item or a player guide. Many primary pages (namu.wiki, the RayCitySEA fandom wiki, web.archive.org) refused automated fetches. In those cases the facts below come from search-engine snippets of those pages and are marked **[snippet]**. Anything marked **[uncertain]** is thin or contradicted.

## 0. Identity and history (for context)

- Developer: J2M Soft (founded Dec 2004; its core team reportedly came from the KartRider team). It later became EA Seoul Studio / EA Korea. It was **not NHN or Ntreev.** Sources: [17173 (2006)](https://news.17173.com/content/2006-12-31/20061231095644611,1.shtml), [ko.wikipedia](https://ko.wikipedia.org/wiki/%EB%A0%88%EC%9D%B4%EC%8B%9C%ED%8B%B0)
- Publishers: Neowiz/Pmang (KR), 9you (CN/HK), Goldensoft (TH), M-etel (TW), and Asiasoft/PlayPark (SEA English, also open to NA/EU). KR launch 2006-12-30, TH 2007-07-07 per ko.wikipedia. A Thai news item has a TH closed beta on 2008-06-16, which conflicts **[uncertain]**. Korean service closed 2013-05-21 and Thailand 2013-11-30. Sources: [ko.wikipedia](https://ko.wikipedia.org/wiki/%EB%A0%88%EC%9D%B4%EC%8B%9C%ED%8B%B0), [MGR Online](https://mgronline.com/game/detail/9510000061973), [MMOHuts review](https://mmohuts.com/news/raycity-review)
- Don't confuse RayCity with **CityRacer (시티레이서)**, an older Korean game with similar ideas. Some search tools mix the two up. ([ko.wikipedia](https://ko.wikipedia.org/wiki/%EB%A0%88%EC%9D%B4%EC%8B%9C%ED%8B%B0))
- An IP-revival claim by "Devonian Studio" exists, but it is promotional and unverified ([gg.deals](https://gg.deals/game/raycity/), not fetched).

## 2. How races start and how you join

- **Open-world 1v1:** you target a driver, they accept, and the track is drawn onto the live streets ([JoongAng Daily](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/business/new-year-off-to-speedy-start-with-multiplayer-race-games/10358124)). It is unclear whether NPC drivers could be challenged this way **[uncertain]**. Road Fight covers NPC rivals.
- **Lobby races:** each hub village has a **racing lobby**, plus garage, shop, gas station, repair shop, open market and skill NPCs. Rooms hold up to 20, and channels are gated by level ([ko.wikipedia](https://ko.wikipedia.org/wiki/%EB%A0%88%EC%9D%B4%EC%8B%9C%ED%8B%B0), [MMOHuts](https://mmohuts.com/news/raycity-review)).
- **Scheduled events (Jilju/Pokju/Survival):** you register at fixed clock times and are sorted into channels of 40 to 100 ([ko.wikipedia](https://ko.wikipedia.org/wiki/%EB%A0%88%EC%9D%B4%EC%8B%9C%ED%8B%B0), [MMOHuts game page](https://mmohuts.com/game/raycity/)).
- **Missions:** you drive up to an NPC client in the field. Party missions require a group ([fandom Newbies Guide [snippet]](https://raycity.fandom.com/wiki/Newbies_Guide), [ko.wikipedia](https://ko.wikipedia.org/wiki/%EB%A0%88%EC%9D%B4%EC%8B%9C%ED%8B%B0)).

## 3. Win conditions and rules

- **Routes:** lobby races use fixed tracks. Field races (1v1, Jilju/Pokju) go between **pre-set points in the city** along roads that the game marks ([Lutris](https://lutris.net/games/raycity/), [JoongAng](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/business/new-year-off-to-speedy-start-with-multiplayer-race-games/10358124)). Whether you could freely shortcut between points in field races was **not documented**.
- **Checkpoints** exist in races and in taxi/checkpoint quests, and passing one **awards SP** (boost energy) [snippet, namu.wiki; Quest Guide].
- **Catch-up (rubber band):** a trailing car gets a speed boost proportional to how far behind it is, and earns more SP per checkpoint [snippet, namu.wiki]. A player guide also says losing pace early is recoverable ([GGemGuide](http://www.ggemguide.com/guide_on_view.htm?uid=3417)). In SEA Survival, players falling behind got an SP boost ([MMOHuts game page](https://mmohuts.com/game/raycity/)).
- **Traffic and collisions:** in Pokju, ramming traffic gives SP (a reward, not a penalty). In taxi missions, near-misses and jumps over cars are rewarded and collisions are penalised ([ko.wikipedia](https://ko.wikipedia.org/wiki/%EB%A0%88%EC%9D%B4%EC%8B%9C%ED%8B%B0), [MMOHuts](https://mmohuts.com/news/raycity-review)). Cars needed **repair**, so damage persisted ([ko.wikipedia](https://ko.wikipedia.org/wiki/%EB%A0%88%EC%9D%B4%EC%8B%9C%ED%8B%B0)).
- **Fuel:** cars use fuel. Gas stations sit in the villages, and lobby RP can buy fuel ([ko.wikipedia](https://ko.wikipedia.org/wiki/%EB%A0%88%EC%9D%B4%EC%8B%9C%ED%8B%B0)).
- **Elimination:** Survival drops the bottom 20% each round ([MMOHuts game page](https://mmohuts.com/game/raycity/)).
- **Not found:** time limits, off-route or wrong-way penalties, and DNF rules. Missions had retries and limits only in CityRacer sources, not RayCity, so don't copy that.

### Boost / SP rules [snippet, namu.wiki 레이시티/스킬]
- Boost is a **skill that costs SP**: 35 SP base, 30 with a booster part fitted.
- You have to keep the accelerator held to sustain it. Releasing cancels it.
- SP refills **over time**. It refills faster through **"thrill combos"** (near-misses past cars) and by picking up **"Lucky Rain"** items, and you also get it at checkpoints.
- In Pokju, buff skills like boost were reportedly nearly ignored (r905 revision) **[uncertain]**.
- Unlockable skills include drift, boost, jump, slide and burnout, plus passive and active buffs. Skills come from quests, levels and skill NPCs ([Lutris](https://lutris.net/games/raycity/), [ko.wikipedia](https://ko.wikipedia.org/wiki/%EB%A0%88%EC%9D%B4%EC%8B%9C%ED%8B%B0)).

## 4. Rewards and progression

- **Two levels:** a player (racer) level and a **car level**, each with its own skills. The player cap was raised to 50 and the car level cap to 200 in 2011 ([ko.wikipedia](https://ko.wikipedia.org/wiki/%EB%A0%88%EC%9D%B4%EC%8B%9C%ED%8B%B0), [GameAbout](http://www.gameabout.com/news/articleView.html?idxno=23146)).
- **Currencies:** **Rain (레인)** comes from quests and races and buys cars and parts. **RP (Racing Points)** comes from lobby races and is exchanged at lobby NPCs for items and fuel. Events ran 200% RP boosts ([MMOHuts](https://mmohuts.com/news/raycity-review), [ko.wikipedia](https://ko.wikipedia.org/wiki/%EB%A0%88%EC%9D%B4%EC%8B%9C%ED%8B%B0), [Ruliweb](https://bbs.ruliweb.com/news/538/read/26911)).
- **EXP:** quests were the easiest source of EXP ([MMOHuts](https://mmohuts.com/news/raycity-review), [Quest Guide [snippet]](https://raycity.fandom.com/wiki/Quest_Guide)).
- **Unlocks:** quests unlock map regions, skills and items. Player level gates which of the 10 Seoul regions you can enter ([Lutris](https://lutris.net/games/raycity/), [ko.wikipedia](https://ko.wikipedia.org/wiki/%EB%A0%88%EC%9D%B4%EC%8B%9C%ED%8B%B0)).
- **Race-count rewards:** item rewards at 5, 10 and 20 races on new tracks. Playing the RC1 track enough unlocked a talisman that turned your car into a formula car, plus an "RC1 Booster" part ([Ruliweb](https://bbs.ruliweb.com/news/538/read/26911), [GameMeca](https://www.gamemeca.com/view.php?gid=90633)).
- **Titles:** beating Road Fight bosses gave a "Road King/Road Fighter" title plus rewards ([Asiae](https://www.asiae.co.kr/article/2010122711252677519)).
- **Licence or rank system:** **not found** in any source.
- **Criticism:** races were "far too gear dependent", so the best car and parts usually won ([MMOHuts](https://mmohuts.com/news/raycity-review)). That is why Survival and RC1 equalised cars.

## 5. Car classes and stats

- **8 classes:** small, compact, mid-size, SUV, full-size, sports, supercar and Legend. Performance gaps grow sharply from supercar up. Legend cars need level 40+, ~50M Rain and a dedicated quest, and cannot be bought with cash ([namu.moe mirror of namu.wiki/차량](https://namu.moe/w/%EB%A0%88%EC%9D%B4%EC%8B%9C%ED%8B%B0/%EC%B0%A8%EB%9F%89)).
- Cars are gated by level and price (Rain or cash), and some also need a quest. Cars had fictional names based on real models. There were **4 starter cars**: Thousand, iSpa, AltoQQ and BNV3 ([namu.moe](https://namu.moe/w/%EB%A0%88%EC%9D%B4%EC%8B%9C%ED%8B%B0/%EC%B0%A8%EB%9F%89), [MMOHuts](https://mmohuts.com/news/raycity-review)).
- **Engines define the league:** Alpha, Beta, Gamma and Theta, and tuning parts only fit the matching engine type ([ko.wikipedia](https://ko.wikipedia.org/wiki/%EB%A0%88%EC%9D%B4%EC%8B%9C%ED%8B%B0)). Other parts included boosters ("Top Speed Booster") and body kits such as the "robot", "wide" and "Tajimah" sets ([GameAbout](http://www.gameabout.com/news/articleView.html?idxno=23146), [GGemGuide](https://www.ggemguide.com/guides/online/1856/)).
- Top speeds were around 311 to 312 km/h at the high end ([MMOHuts game page](https://mmohuts.com/game/raycity/), [GGemGuide stock car](http://www.ggemguide.com/guide_on_view.htm?uid=3417)). I found no named stat list (speed, accel, handling) **[not documented]**.

## 6. City map design

- Seoul was rebuilt from **real geographic data**, with real roads, buildings, street signs and real company signs (some of them paid product placement) ([JoongAng](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/business/new-year-off-to-speedy-start-with-multiplayer-race-games/10358124), [MMOHuts game page](https://mmohuts.com/game/raycity/)). The map launched with southern and downtown Seoul, and later neighbourhoods were added. It was described as a scaled-down recreation.
- The map has **10 field regions** across the Gangnam, Seocho, Jung, Jongno, Seongdong, Dongdaemun, Gwangjin, Songpa and Gangdong districts, plus a fictional "Dark City". Access to regions is gated by level ([ko.wikipedia](https://ko.wikipedia.org/wiki/%EB%A0%88%EC%9D%B4%EC%8B%9C%ED%8B%B0)).
- There are **5 hub villages** (COEX, Dogok-dong, Namsan, Hanyang Univ., Saha Tunnel/Dark City), each with all the services in one place. Quest givers sit at real landmarks and intersections (Sinsa Station, Seoul Station and others) ([ko.wikipedia](https://ko.wikipedia.org/wiki/%EB%A0%88%EC%9D%B4%EC%8B%9C%ED%8B%B0), [GGemGuide](https://www.ggemguide.com/guides/online/1856/)).
- Time of day matters: dawn, day, evening and night were added in 2011, and they drive the Jilju (day) vs Pokju (night) event split. Lobby tracks were real places too: Gwanghwamun to Itaewon, Cheonggyecheon, and **Busan** (Gwangan Bridge, the harbour), each map with a fixed time of day ([GameAbout](http://www.gameabout.com/news/articleView.html?idxno=23146), [Ruliweb](https://bbs.ruliweb.com/news/538/read/26911)).
- Clicking the map gave **auto-navigation** to a destination ([MMOHuts](https://mmohuts.com/news/raycity-review)).

## 7. Recommendations for MIDNIGHT PATTAYA (then called Pattaya Racing World)

These race types use our existing systems: the open-city map built from real roads, checkpoint races with laps, and raycast arcade cars with nitro and drift.

**Phase 1: build first**
1. **Street Challenge (1v1 to 4, open city).** Our take on the RayCity on-road request. Click or target a nearby player, or press a "flash" key while behind them; we can add the headlight flash as our own flavour. They get 10 s to accept. The server picks a point-to-point route of 3 to 8 checkpoints from the road graph, starting where the cars are, then draws the route as gates and arrows on the live street. Countdown, then race. Win: first to the final checkpoint. Checkpoints must be passed in order, and a missed one shows "wrong way" and has to be retaken. DNF after 3 minutes, or 60 s after the winner finishes. Traffic stays on.
2. **Circuit Race (lobby, 2 to 12).** Reuses our checkpoint/lap system on closed loops: Beach Road and Second Road loop, Walking Street, and the Pratamnak hill. Fixed laps, a ghost reset to the last checkpoint after 5 s off-road, no traffic. This is our RayCity "speed race".
3. **Nitro as SP.** Follow RayCity's tested design. The nitro meter refills slowly over time, **+chunk per checkpoint**, +bonus for drift chains and near-misses with traffic ("thrill combo"). Boost only stays on while throttle is held. In races, give trailing players more nitro per checkpoint (soft catch-up), not a raw speed boost.
4. **Rewards.** Money (our "Rain") plus EXP for every race, scaled by finishing position and number of entrants. A small daily first-win bonus. Taxi and delivery jobs are the main EXP source, as in RayCity.

**Phase 2**
5. **Taxi / Delivery jobs.** An NPC at a landmark (Central Pattaya, Terminal 21, Bali Hai pier) gives a timed A-to-B run, optionally through checkpoints. Near-misses and jumps add a tip, and each collision cuts the pay.
6. **Scheduled "Night Run" (Pokju).** Mass event every N real minutes for the whole server on a long city point-to-point route (Jomtien to Naklua). Ramming traffic earns nitro. On weekends it becomes **Survival**: 3 to 5 rounds, bottom 20% eliminated each round.
7. **Class races / equalised races.** Group cars into tiers (like Alpha to Theta) and only allow same-tier entrants, or run "spec" events with a speed cap and parts disabled. This addresses RayCity's main criticism that races were too gear-dependent.
8. **Road Fight bosses.** One NPC "Road Master" per district (Jomtien, South, Central, North, Naklua, Pratamnak), then a final "Road King". Uses the same Street Challenge code with an AI rival. Rewards are titles and cars.

**Design notes from RayCity**
- Unlock districts by level, place hubs (garage, shop, race lobby) at real landmarks, and put the day/night cycle into event types.
- Skip fuel, repair costs and persistent damage at first. They are friction that Roblox players may not tolerate.
- Show only the next 2 checkpoints and a minimap route line. RayCity's auto-navigation suggests a GPS line works well in a real-road map.
